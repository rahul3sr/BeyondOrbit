# Executive Summary / Direct Answer

The strongest fully reviewed tutorial is **“How To Actually Make Money AI Clipping”** by Artem: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_8qFChfsENQ (9:43).

It wins because it rejects the simplistic “post clips and get rich” model and lays out three progressively stronger businesses: campaign payouts, creator retainers, then content strategy/original production. Beyond Orbit should adopt that ladder.

Viewing verdict: **watch 0:00–4:20 and 9:17–9:34, or use this summary.** The middle creator-service section is useful but can be summarized. Treat Everygen demonstrations and income ranges as vendor/creator marketing, not forecasts.

# Core Findings

- AI reduces editing labor but also lowers barriers for competitors.
- Views have value only when tied to a payout, client outcome, or owned audience asset.
- Campaign clipping is useful as a proof-building launch wedge.
- Creator retainers improve revenue recurrence.
- Strategy/original production creates the most defensible value.
- Human judgment remains necessary for the hook; the tutorial’s own example improves when the AI-selected clip is manually trimmed.

# What Matters Most

Beyond Orbit should not automate publishing merely because a clipping engine produces candidates. The durable system is campaign/rights intake → human hook judgment → exact-file approval → controlled publication → measured contribution.

# Consensus and Important Disagreements

The three-level business model is supported directionally by current Content Rewards and Vyro campaign workflows and by the structural economics of fixed retainers. Specific monthly-income claims are not independently supported.

# Claims That Did Not Hold Up

- “AI makes clipping 95% easier/faster”: plausible marketing estimate, unverified.
- “A million monthly views is definitely possible”: possibility is not a probability or forecast.
- $10K–$20K clipper months and $50K–$100K agencies: unverified anecdotes/ranges.
- Five clients can be served without “crazy hours”: depends on quality, approval, platform, and client-management scope.

# Best Insights

The most important insight is not the editing tool. It is the revenue ladder: campaign proof → recurring creator service → outcome-linked strategy and original production.

# Best Videos to Watch

**Best overall:** “How To Actually Make Money AI Clipping,” Artem, 9:43. Full transcript reviewed from auto-captions. Provisional watch score: 69/100; useful but commercially biased. Watch 0:00–4:20 and 9:17–9:34.

# Risks / Caveats

The creator promotes Everygen, the companion article is published by Everygen, and the precise earnings ranges were not evidenced. The video barely addresses copyright, platform reused-content rules, disclosure requirements, payout caps, or account eligibility. Those gaps are central for Beyond Orbit.

# Final Takeaway

Use the video’s business ladder, not its revenue promises. Pair it with official campaign, copyright, and platform-monetization rules. Beyond Orbit’s operating map now does that.
